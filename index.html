Landscapes of Injustice and Past Wrongs Future Choices

Partnership Research at the University of Victoria

Spanning 2013 to 2029, two linked research projects at the University of Victoria examined the 1940s dispossession of Japanese Canadians and placed it within the global story of Nikkei uprooting.


This site serves as an entry point to the varied results of our work.
